## Visual identity (match Persistent template)

### Colors

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Navy (primary background)** | `#0D2137` | Title slide, close slide backgrounds |
| **Orange (accent)** | `#E85D04` | Section labels, top bars, arrows, highlights, team name |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Content slide backgrounds, title text on navy |
| **Dark blue (headings)** | `#1A2B4A` | H1/H2 on white slides |
| **Body text** | `#333333` | Paragraphs and bullets |
| **Peach / coral box** | `#F8E8DE` | “Evidence”, “differentiators”, highlight panels |
| **Light blue box** | `#E3F2FD` | “Speaker cue”, tips, info callouts |
| **Light gray border** | `#D0D0D0` | Column/card outlines |
| **Success green** | `#2EB886` | Pass metrics in charts (optional) |
| **Fail red** | `#E81123` | Fail metrics (optional) |

### Typography

- **Headings:** Bold sans-serif (Arial, Segoe UI, or Inter) — corporate, clean  
- **Section labels:** ALL CAPS, orange, small (e.g. `NEED`, `APPROACH`, `DEMO`)  
- **Body:** Regular sans-serif, 18–22pt equivalent on content slides  
- **Footer:** 10–12pt gray `#666666` — left: `Semicolons 2026 | Team Dhurandhar` — right: slide number  

### Layout rules

1. **Thin orange horizontal bar** at very top of every content slide (2–4px).  
2. **Title slide:** Split layout — **left 55% navy** with text; **right 45%** photo (developer at laptop, code on screen).  
3. **Content slides:** White background, generous padding, max 6 bullets per block.  
4. **Time badges:** Small orange rectangle top-right (`1 min`, `2 min`, `5 min`) where specified.  
5. **Boxes:** Rounded corners 8px; peach or light blue fills for callouts.  
6. **Arrows:** Thick orange for flow diagrams (architecture, demo storyboard).  
7. **Do not** use playful/cartoon style — **enterprise consulting** tone.

When Gamma offers **AI images**, use:

**Style keywords:** professional, corporate technology, fintech, minimal, high contrast, no cartoon, photographic or clean flat illustration

- Developer at laptop with terminal/code (navy + orange lighting)
